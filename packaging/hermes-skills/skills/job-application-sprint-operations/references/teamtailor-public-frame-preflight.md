2. Project candidate-visible labels, required markers, options and submit-adjacent consent. Do not enter candidate data, accept permissions or call candidate submission routes.

4. Preserve explicit no-other-ongoing-jobs exclusivity attestations unanswered. A global contractor label does not authorize that commitment, and silence in canon establishes neither Yes nor No.
5. A Locations Others option does not establish employment-country acceptance. Keep unanswered geography, consent and specialist tooling gates separate from verified employer headquarters.
