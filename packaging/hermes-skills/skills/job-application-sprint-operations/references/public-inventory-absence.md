# Public inventories and exact-role absence

## Department headings misrepresented as vacancies

Require a current named role and its own employer-controlled detail link before promoting a discovery mirror. Mirrors can index a department heading such as Customer Success and describe the whole careers board as a remote job. Preserve the exact mirror and current official inventory separately; inspect the real role cards and their location/work-mode labels rather than inventing a department-level vacancy, assuming the mirror is closed employer-wide, or substituting a general intake. A worldwide distributed team does not override a current Israel Hybrid or USA Remote card.
