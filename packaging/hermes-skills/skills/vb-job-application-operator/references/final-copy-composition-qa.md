# Final composed-copy QA

1. Validate the complete selected body after paragraph selection, not only each source revision. Whole immutable model paragraphs preserve provenance but do not establish final acceptance.
2. Skip the greeting, normalize apostrophes and contractions, then reject generic first topic leads such as `I am applying`, `I'm applying`, `I am writing to apply` and `I'm writing to apply` unless the exact application field forces very short plain text. Request a new complete model paragraph; never write candidate prose as an operator repair.
3. Recheck role-specific motivation, every required answer, all contacts, location, chronology, complete signature, length and sentence completeness after omissions or combinations.
4. Keep internal safety-only gap and negative-nonclaim analysis outside Teal Notes. Preserve exact employer requirements and supported candidate evidence in the tracker. A fit map copied from internal research can unintentionally volunteer nonclaims even when the email is clean.
5. If an already-sent truthful message has only a banal lead or other minor stylistic defect, preserve the actual Sent text, log the deviation and never resend without scoped authorization. Repair tracker-only internal commentary with an exact note readback while preserving role, source, selected status, provider application date and follow-up date.
6. Do not convert end-to-end engagement ownership into unsupported exclusivity or staffing claims. Owning discovery, pricing, delivery and monitoring does not establish a one-person studio, no team handoffs, sole commercial ownership or AI replacing a team. Reject those comparisons before sending and request one compact positive paragraph from an exact activity whitelist; preserve safe complete source paragraphs instead of repeatedly regenerating the whole application.
