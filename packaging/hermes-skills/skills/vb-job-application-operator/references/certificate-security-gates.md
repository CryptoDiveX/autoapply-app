1. Treat a native certificate-security interstitial, including `NET::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID`, as a security gate. Preserve the exact observed employer URL and error without entering candidate data.
2. Do not choose Advanced or unsafe Proceed, downgrade to HTTP, disable certificate verification, change browser security flags or send files through that route. A plausible search-indexed vacancy is not proof of a safe current intake.
3. Recover only through an exact currently observed employer-controlled valid application route. A parent-brand relationship or similarly named domain does not establish an alternative hostname or transferable candidate authorization. Inspect the actual role, geography, required questions, consents and transmission path again before promotion.
4. Keep unresolved certificate routes research-only and non-counted. Close only their exact owned research tabs, retain evidence and follow-up ownership, and move to unrelated eligible employers rather than retrying unchanged warnings.
