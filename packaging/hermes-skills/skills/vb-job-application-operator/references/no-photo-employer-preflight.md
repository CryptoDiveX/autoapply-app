# Employer no-photo candidate-data instructions

1. Read candidate-specific recruitment privacy instructions before drafting or sending. A generic current privacy notice can explicitly request that candidates do not provide photos even when the careers page invites emailed resumes.
2. The approved immutable PDF contains a headshot. If an employer explicitly asks candidates not to provide photos, defer the route unless Vasya has approved an exact compliant alternative artifact.
3. Do not remove the headshot, rebuild the PDF, send the unchanged photo resume against the employer's instruction, or silently replace the application with an unsolicited link-only message.
4. Preserve the exact employer page, wording and source URL. Log the document mismatch as preflight-only with no application credit, then continue to another eligible employer.
