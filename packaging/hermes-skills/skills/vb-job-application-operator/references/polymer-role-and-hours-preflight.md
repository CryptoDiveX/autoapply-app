# Polymer exact-role and working-hours preflight

1. Follow the currently observed role link from the employer's official careers page. A current Client Partner opening can have a different numeric Polymer ID from a search-indexed Project Manager mirror. Preserve both identifiers and do not transfer the old role's geography or requirements.
2. Inspect the full settled native role description AND the separate Remote restrictions section near Apply here before copywriting or candidate entry. The description can state one New York overlap minimum while the native restrictions specify a different minimum with Monterrey, Mexico. Preserve the exact cities, hours and wording independently.
3. Do not treat work from anywhere as eliminating mandatory working-hours overlap. Canonical workable morning overlap with North America does not establish a daily five- or six-hour commitment, and Canadian citizenship does not establish current North American residence. Defer an unsupported commitment rather than invent availability.
4. Use visible required labels and the current published form schema or ordinary validation rather than input.required alone. Native Polymer controls can expose required=false while the rendered labels say required.
5. Preserve normal anti-abuse, consent, approved-file and submission-proof gates. A reCAPTCHA footer alone is not proof of a visible challenge; never invoke or rely on a solver, and do not enter a challenged flow to test whether one will complete it. Do not infer a completed application from selected-file state or a generic success route.
