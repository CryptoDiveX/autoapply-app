# Deel current careers inventory

1. Follow the current official https://www.deel.com/careers/ board before using search-cached Ashby vacancy links. Preserve each exact historical vacancy separately. A native Ashby Job not found result closes that exact route, not the employer's current hiring inventory.
2. Department sections can be collapsed and expose no job links in the initial DOM snapshot. Wait for document readiness and client hydration, click the exact visible `button[id^=career-dept]`, then require its `aria-expanded` to become `true` and read its live panel. A click before hydration can leave the section collapsed. Recover only read-only expansion after a bounded settle, not an empty-board classification.
3. Preserve observed current `/careers/job/<id>/` URLs from that expanded official panel. Do not construct replacements or assume all listings remain on Ashby. Retain pagination limits: an expanded panel may expose only an initial subset of its declared total.
4. Read the exact role's labeled `Locations:` section. Company-wide work-from-anywhere language and a Multiple Locations card do not override a role restricted to specific countries. A public EMEA onboarding role can list Serbia, Poland, Croatia, Albania and North Macedonia without Georgia. Canadian citizenship does not establish Canadian residence.
5. Inspect the current Application tab, candidate questions, terms, file transmission and consent only after country and duplicate gates pass. Public-board discovery is not candidate entry or submission. Preserve old Applied roles and archived blockers without replaying applications or mutating trackers just because the careers platform changed.
