# Devexperts native consent preflight

1. Inspect the exact native form before candidate entry or copywriting. The required `consentSmartCRM` application-privacy checkbox and required `consentSmartRecruiters` checkbox have distinct scopes.
2. Preserve the second label: `By checking this box, I declare that I have read and agree to the privacy policy of Devexperts and agree to be part of communities for future career opportunities.` Both `required=true` and `data-required-field=true` can establish its requiredness. Routine processing authorization does not establish approval for future-community enrollment. Defer without candidate entry unless this exact scope is authorized.
3. Keep separate optional `newsletter` unchecked. Do not conflate optional general materials with required career-community enrollment or label both marketing opt-ins.
4. Evaluate exact roles independently. A dxFeed Application Support Engineer can offer remote work in Georgia and basic technical requirements while another Devexperts Business Analyst requires specialist trading and Oracle experience. Preserve subsidiary identity and official country evidence without inheriting another role's qualifications.
5. Save native role/consent evidence and non-submitted tracker status/date/Notes after scoped tracker writes. Do not bypass required consent through another inbox or API.
