# Comparative screening facts

1. Treat questions asking which coding tool was used most heavily, which language was shipped most often, or whether personally shipped code falls inside a specific recent period as distinct factual claims. A supported tool list, daily Python use or aggregate production-code years does not establish a comparative winner or the requested recent window.
2. Preserve exact live wording and options, consult current canon and Q&A, and defer an unsupported required choice before candidate entry, upload or copywriting. Do not choose the most plausible tool or convert delivery leadership into personally written production code.
3. Keep the verified career record separate from missing comparative facts. Record the precise unresolved question, not an invented positive claim or unsupported negative answer.
